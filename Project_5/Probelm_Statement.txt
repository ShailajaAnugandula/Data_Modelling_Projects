Problem Statement:
------------------
A multinational retail company has successfully completed the 
dimensional modeling phase of its enterprise Data Warehouse. 
The management now requires the design team to implement a Star 
Schema that can efficiently support Business Intelligence (BI) and 
Online Analytical Processing (OLAP) queries.

The organization receives sales transactions daily from multiple 
retail branches located across India. Business analysts frequently 
generate reports such as Top Customers, Top Products, Monthly Revenue,
Branch Performance, State-wise Sales, Category-wise Revenue, Customer 
Purchase Trends, and Product Performance Dashboards.

To improve query performance and simplify analytical reporting, the 
Data Warehouse Architect has decided to organize the warehouse using 
the Star Schema architecture.

As a Data Warehouse Designer, your responsibility is to identify the 
Fact Table, Dimension Tables, Primary Keys, Foreign Keys, measures, 
and relationships, and then design a complete Star Schema capable of 
supporting all required analytical reports.
